Esper Blink took 43 challenge-class lists this week, 9.6% of every published top-32 slot, up from 7.5% the week before. Its fourth straight week of growth.

Against its own post-regime history the deck is at four times baseline: the median week since the ban is 10 challenge-class lists. Volume like this is a deck being copied as much as a deck being good, and the figures below should be read that way until a quiet week tests them.

It is converting above its presence. 17 lists made top 8, 15.2% of that band, against 9.6% of the top 32, and 26 made top 16. The gap has held every week since 10 August rather than appearing once.

The fortnight to 23 August was the busiest for deckbuilding since the ban. Clarion Conqueror went from 61% to 99% of sideboards across 112 lists, which is now effectively a fifth maindeck slot. Containment Priest came back after 24 weeks away at 21 lists, and Geist of Saint Traft arrived new at 6. The first two are graveyard hate landing together, which is the field answering something.

Orzhov Blink remains marginal: 2 lists this week, both challenge-class, no trophies.
