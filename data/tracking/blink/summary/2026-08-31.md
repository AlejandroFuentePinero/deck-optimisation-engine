Two Esper Blink pilots made the top 32 at Spotlight Dallas: evilgorrilaz 19th and littlesir 20th, both at 11-4.

On MTGO, Esper Blink achieved 43 finishes in swiss-like tournaments this week, 9.6% of every published top-32 slot, up from 7.5% the week before. That is its fourth straight week of growth.

Against its own history since the Modern bans it is at four times baseline, the median week being 10 finishes. It is level against where it has just been, though: the four weeks behind this one ran a median of 30. The deck is holding a new level rather than climbing onto one, and the report no longer flags the volume.

It is converting above its presence. 17 lists made top 8, 15.2% of that band against 9.6% of the top 32, and 26 made top 16. The gap has held every week since 10 August rather than appearing once.

The fortnight to 6 September rebuilt the sideboard rather than the deck. Nihil Spellbomb went from 9% to 58% of sideboards across 133 lists, White Orchid Phantom from 31% to 61% and Elesh Norn, Mother of Machines from 6% to 38%. Damping Sphere fell from 76% to 22% and Mystical Dispute from 71% to 41%. Consign to Memory changed boards, 10% to 41% of mainboards and 90% to 59% of sideboards.

Behind those two finishes, Dallas was 61 Esper lists of a 928-list field, 6.6% of it, against 11 of 571 (1.9%) at Spotlight Brisbane the week before. Its pilots went 296-244-27, a 52.2% match win rate against a field at 48.8%. It held 2 of the top 32 at 0.95 times its field share, where Brisbane held 1 at 1.62 times and finished 2nd. 47 of the 61 mainboarded Consign to Memory, which is the fortnight's move above arriving in paper at the same time.

Orzhov Blink remains marginal: 2 lists on MTGO this week, both in swiss-like tournaments, no trophies.
