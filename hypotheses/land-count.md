## Evidence sought

The 75 is on twenty-two. The claim is the cut to twenty-one, which is the
direction the question has to be asked from: a hypothesis that the manabase is
already right is one no evidence can contradict.

A deck-level configuration, read exactly as a card's is: the camp's lists
across the counts they registered, in one stratum, over the fresh window. The
land count moving is the same kind of decision as a copy crossing into the
sideboard.

The delta is the reading that matters here rather than the share. A camp
sitting on twenty-two and a camp that has just arrived there from twenty-one
are the same number and opposite news, and the manabase is the slot the field
drifts on most quietly. The performance tilt is the other half: a count the
camp has just moved to that finishes behind the camp is the herd adopting an
underperformer, which is what the manabase drifting would look like.

Which land the twenty-second one is is a separate argument; that is the
green-source record.
