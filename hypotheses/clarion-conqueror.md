# Two Clarion Conqueror belong in the sideboard against the combo decks

status: open
origin: pilot-experience
subject: Clarion Conqueror

## Evidence sought

The card is bought against Devoted Combo and Gruul Basking Broodscale, and it
is paid for twice: once in the two slots, and once in what it does to the
Psychic Frog. That second cost is the whole of why this is a record rather than
a slot decision, because no published list reports it. A pilot who registers
the card and keeps four Frogs has either decided the tension is survivable or
has not met it, and the decklist cannot tell those apart.

Conditional on combo density, which the header cannot say. The only condition
the schema carries is the mirror, and this claim is not bought against the
mirror at all, so the combo share has to be read into the log by hand on every
entry. A slot bought on Devoted plus Broodscale being an eighth of the field is
unbought the week that recedes.

Three readings are wanted, in this order.

What the camp does with it, which is the cheap one and is already thin: five
lists, three pilots, a fortnight old. Adoption is the only instrument with any
power at this population and it answers a different question from the claim.

Whether it survives its own fortnight. The ledger is watching a breakthrough
flag on the 2026-08-11 league list and wants two followers by 2026-08-25. That
is the field's verdict rather than five pilots' opinion, and it lands two days
before submission. It is the strongest thing this data will produce on the card
and it may well come back empty.

What the two slots displace, which the camp cannot answer because the 75 is not
where the camp is. Nothing in the sideboard is spare, so this record is not
settled until it names what goes, and the honest candidates are on the axis the
postboard-countermagic record is already arguing about.

### 2026-08-12 judgment

Clarion Conqueror could be an interesting sideboard addition to deal with Broodscale and Devoted Druid combo, but it interacts badly with our own Frogs, so there is a lot of tension in the slot. The tension is the claim's whole difficulty and nothing published reports it: all five adopters kept four Psychic Frog, four Ephemerate and four Goryo's Vengeance, so not one of them paid for the interaction in the maindeck, and their lists cannot say whether they judged it survivable or never met it.

### 2026-08-14 data

The combo density this slot is bought against was understated by the site's own archetype split, and the correction roughly doubles it. MTGGoldfish tables the Broodscale deck twice, as Eldrazi and as Gruul Basking Broodscale Combo; the pilot checked the cards and they are one deck, so the engine now merges the two rows across the whole snapshot history. Read that way, Broodscale is 7.4, 7.7, 8.2, 7.7, 7.5 percent over the five 14-day snapshots, not the 2.3 to 3.0 the earlier entries quote, and it is the field's second deck behind the mirror on every one of them. Combo density for this record, Devoted Combo plus Broodscale, is therefore 13.5 percent as of 2026-08-14 (6.0 and 7.5) against the 8.4 percent the 08-12 entries carried, and it has been above 13 on every snapshot since 08-11. That is more of the field than the mirror.
