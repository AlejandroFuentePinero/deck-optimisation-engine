status: open

## Evidence sought

The green is there to cast Atraxa and nothing else, so one source is the whole
commitment and which one it is is a manabase decision rather than a colour one.
The 75 is on Breeding Pool.

What the camp registers is the reading, and it is the kind of slot the camp may
not have argued about at all: a single land nobody thinks about is exactly
where a whole camp can be copying rather than choosing. So a share here is read
with the pet-tech and hype flags beside it, and a camp unanimous on the shock
land is not on its own an answer.

What is at stake is what the land costs on the turn it comes down against what
it costs over the game, in a deck that pays life to its own manabase already
and wants the green source on a specific turn rather than early. That is a
playtesting question, not an adoption one.
