# Two Spell Snare and three Mystical Dispute is too much postboard countermagic

status: open
origin: pilot-experience
subject: Mystical Dispute
subject: Spell Snare
conditional-on: mirror

## Evidence sought

Five sideboard cards on the same axis. What the camp sides against blue and in
what mix is the first reading, since a camp on three where the 75 is on five is
the camp saying the fifth card is not wanted.

Conditional, because countermagic postboard is bought against the blue half of
the field and the mirror is the largest part of it: five slots at ten percent
mirror and five at half of that are different decisions, and only the second
one is obviously wrong. So the camp's counts are read against the mirror share
that stood on the day, and the claim is only ever true of a meta.

The two cards are not interchangeable inside the five. Which of them the camp
cuts first, if it cuts, is what says whether the answer is fewer counterspells
or a different mix of them.
