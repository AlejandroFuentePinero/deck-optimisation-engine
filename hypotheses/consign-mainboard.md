claim moves one copy of Consign into the mainboard over the third Force, and it

it, and what they then do with the slot that frees. A camp that mains a Consign
and keeps four in the 75 has not freed anything, and the second half of the
claim would be wrong even where the first half is right.

The third Force of Negation is also what the fourth-thoughtseize record wants
to spend. The two argue over the same slot, and both cannot be acted on.
