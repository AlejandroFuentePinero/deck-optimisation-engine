## Evidence sought

Two questions that have to be read together, which is why they share a record.
How many sweepers the 75 sides is the first: the current package is three
cards, and both two and three are live answers. Which cards they are is the
second, and the argument there is a third Wrath of the Skies against the Pest
Control that holds the slot now.

Read as one card each, the two subjects would answer separately and neither
answer would be about the package. What the claim is about is the sum and the
mix at once, so the camp's counts of both are the one reading.

The performance tilt on each configuration is what tells a camp that has
settled from a camp that has merely copied. A configuration the camp is on that
finishes behind the camp is the herd adopting an underperformer, and a
sideboard slot nobody argues about is exactly where that happens.
